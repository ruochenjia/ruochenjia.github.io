The domain unbioctium.com

You may be wondering why I chose the domain unbioctium.com instead of ruochenjia.com for my personal website.
The reason is pretty simple: At the start of 2024, I was planning for a more ambitious project after my previous project (NettleWeb) received a good popularity. I reserved the domain unbioctium.com becase the word 'unbioctium' is the systematic name for the 128th chemical element. Back to the early 1990s, 32-bit computers just became dominant and replaced 16-bit computers; in late 2000s to early 2010s, 64-bit became the mainstream. So I believe one day 128-bit will eventually take over 64-bit, in which the name 'unbioctium' would be a good starting point.
However the project I planned to be hosted on the domain unbioctium.com was ultimately abandoned, mainly due to its complexity and limitations of my coding skills at the time. As the result, the domain became parked for nearly two years, although it was still used as the backend and API endpoint for my other projects.
Therefore I decided to give the domain a better use -- my personal website, although this is temporary and the domain is still reserved for my future projects. (In which if this did happen, my personal website will be moved to ruochenjia.github.io or ruochenjia.pages.dev)
